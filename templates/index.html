How It Works

1. Create an Event

Event organizers select a food truck, choose delicious menu items with markup pricing, and launch their community fundraising campaign.

2. Community Rallies

Food lovers pledge for meal e-tickets that support their community. Payments are secure and only charged when goals are met.

3. Food Truck Arrives!

Goals achieved means delicious food served! E-tickets are delivered, and the community celebrates together with amazing meals.
